Business Intelligence Dashboard

Sales, profit and customer analytics from both datasets in one place. Use the tabs, then the filters on the left.

Two datasets, one dashboard. The sales dataset (8,000 orders, Jan 2024 to 15 May 2025) feeds Overview, Sales & Profit. The customer dataset (8,000 customers) feeds Customers. They share no customer ID, and matching on name is unreliable (only 612 names appear in both and names repeat inside each file), so filters apply within each dataset and are not combined.

Definitions. Sales is revenue per order, Profit is Sales minus Expense, and Margin is Profit divided by Sales. May 2025 covers 1 to 15 May only. Customer spending is the Total_Spending column of the customer dataset.
